# Kyoto 2023

## Fushimi Inari at dawn
city: Kyoto
type: sight
verdict: loved
rating: 5
why: Thousands of red gates with almost nobody there before 7 am, peaceful hike up the hill.
tip: Start at 6 am and walk past the first crowded section.

## Nishiki Market
city: Kyoto
type: activity
verdict: liked
rating: 4
why: Fun to graze on small snacks like tamagoyaki and pickles, but narrow and busy.
tip: Go before lunch and bring cash.

## Arashiyama bamboo grove
city: Kyoto
type: sight
verdict: disliked
rating: 2
why: Much smaller than in photos and shoulder to shoulder with tour groups.
tip: Skip it or go very early; the nearby Okochi Sanso garden is calmer.

## Tofu restaurant near Nanzen-ji
city: Kyoto
type: restaurant
verdict: loved
rating: 5
why: Simple yudofu set menu in a quiet garden, the calmest meal of the trip.
tip: Book lunch, dinner is much pricier.

## Overall
Kyoto rewards early mornings and quiet gardens. Avoid the famous spots at midday.
