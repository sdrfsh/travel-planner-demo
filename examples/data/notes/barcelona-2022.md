# Barcelona 2022

## Bunkers del Carmel
city: Barcelona
type: viewpoint
verdict: loved
rating: 5
why: 360 degree view over the whole city and the sea, best at sunset with a picnic.
tip: Take bus V17, then walk 10 minutes uphill.

## Sagrada Familia
city: Barcelona
type: sight
verdict: loved
rating: 5
why: The light through the stained glass inside is unforgettable.
tip: Book a morning slot online weeks ahead.

## Paella on La Rambla
city: Barcelona
type: restaurant
verdict: hated
rating: 1
why: Microwaved frozen paella at triple the price, worst meal of the trip.
tip: Eat in Gracia or Poble-sec instead.

## Bar in Poble-sec
city: Barcelona
type: restaurant
verdict: liked
rating: 4
why: Cheap pintxos and vermouth with locals, lively but not touristy.
tip: Go around 8 pm on a weekday.

## Overall
Barcelona was great for views and architecture; food is good only away from La Rambla.
