# Rome 2025

## Giardino degli Aranci
city: Rome
type: viewpoint
verdict: loved
rating: 5
why: Quiet orange garden with a wide view over the city at sunset, almost no crowds.
tip: Go 30 minutes before sunset and bring water.

## Trattoria Da Lucia
city: Rome
type: restaurant
verdict: loved
rating: 5
why: Small family place in Trastevere, simple pasta done perfectly, friendly staff.
tip: Book a table; it fills up by 8 pm.

## Trevi Fountain at noon
city: Rome
type: sight
verdict: disliked
rating: 2
why: Beautiful but so packed that I could not get close or enjoy it.
tip: Go before 7 am instead.

## Overall
Rome was best on foot and early in the day. Small family restaurants beat the famous ones.
